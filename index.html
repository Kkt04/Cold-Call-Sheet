Cold call sheet

How to use this
  1. Add every new request with + New job. Paste the customer's text and the phone number fills in.
  2. Start your day at the top of Call these first. The color and label show how urgent each one is.
  3. After you call, tap Called, no change. When a job moves forward, tap its next-step button.
